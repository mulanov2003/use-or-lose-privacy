Use or Lose Privacy

Use or Lose is made by Mark Ulanov.

Your list stays on your iPhone. The app never sends your list anywhere. There is no account, and the app does not connect to your bank.

Like any app, your list is included in your iPhone’s own iCloud or computer backup.

The camera is optional. If you take or choose a photo, or type a description, the app reads it on your phone so you can check the details. The photo and the text are not kept.

Reminders show the item and the amount. To hide them until you unlock your phone, use Settings › Notifications › Show Previews.

App lock protects the app itself. Widgets are hidden while your phone is locked.

The paid unlock is purchased through Apple. Apple handles the payment.

Use or Lose Support

What is this app?

It tracks value you already have — card perks, FSA, gift cards, PTO — so you don’t forget to use it before it disappears.

How does it know my amounts?

It doesn’t — you enter them. We show the usual amounts for common cards as a starting point.

What happens when something resets?

The app refills the amount automatically based on the dates you set.

Are reminders free?

Yes, always. Reminders work for every item, free or paid plan.

Where is my data?

On this phone. The app never sends your list anywhere. Like any app, your list is included in your iPhone’s own iCloud or computer backup.
